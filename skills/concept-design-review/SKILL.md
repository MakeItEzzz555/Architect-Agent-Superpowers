# Concept Design Review

Evaluate against explicit project criteria. Consider relevant program fit, site response, arrival/circulation, accessibility, daylight/views/privacy, climate, massing, structural/grid plausibility, services, envelope/material logic, flexibility, constructability, complexity/cost proxies, and planning risks.

For each issue or option provide observation, evidence, consequence, design opportunity, and confidence. For multiple schemes use a trade-off matrix tied to stated criteria. Do not silently declare a universal best option or redesign major portions unless asked.
