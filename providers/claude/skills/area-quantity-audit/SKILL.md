# Area & Quantity Audit

Establish units and measurement basis. Keep source values separate from derived values. Use deterministic calculations for repeated arithmetic. Preserve precision until presentation. Reconcile subtotals and detect missing IDs, duplicates, blanks, impossible values, and unit mismatches.

Never mix area definitions. Label the exact basis and inclusions/exclusions. Treat drawing/model takeoffs as estimates unless source reliability is established. State exclusions and confidence; provide CSV/JSON when useful downstream.
