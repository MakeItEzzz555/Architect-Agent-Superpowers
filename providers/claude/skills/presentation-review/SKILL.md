# Architecture Presentation Review

Review narrative sequence, hierarchy, one-purpose-per-board/slide, legibility at final size, consistent typography/scale bars/north/legends/labels, redundancy, unsupported claims, visual balance, and consistency across plans, sections, renders, diagrams, and narrative.

Produce a prioritized edit list and concise replacement copy when requested. Do not hide unresolved technical issues behind presentation polish.
